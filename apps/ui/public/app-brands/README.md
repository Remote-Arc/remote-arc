# Product logo sources and trademark status

The product-icon files are **real product brand marks** (not hand-drawn approximations), stored locally for predictable availability and to avoid tracking/calls to external CDNs when a visitor opens our site.

- Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, GitHub, Notion, Google Drive, Airtable, Salesforce and Discord: [Iconify `logos` collection](https://icon-sets.iconify.design/logos/) (SVG Logos collection, Gil Barbara / SVG Logos contributors).
- Google Sheets, Google Docs: [Homarr Dashboard Icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons).

Do not imply endorsement, affiliation, live integration or partnership. Each company owns its trademarks. The marketing UI explicitly labels the 16 logos as **future integration targets**, not available connectors. Brand artwork may change; verify against the product's current brand guidelines before major campaigns or public launch.

Linear and HubSpot use their official vector marks from the [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) archive, colored to maintain brand recognition in dark UI.

## Logo corrections verified on 2026-10-17

- Jira and Confluence now use the current, unmodified app icon artwork from the [official Atlassian logo downloads](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos), replacing the older standalone gradient symbols.
- Microsoft Outlook now uses Microsoft’s 2025 app artwork, replacing the 2019 icon. [Microsoft’s announcement](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365insiderblog/new-microsoft-365-icons-for-the-ai-era/4458674) confirms the refresh; the SVG is [archived on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Microsoft_Outlook_Icon_(2025%E2%80%93present).svg) with Microsoft listed as author and microsoft.com as its original source.
- SVG artwork remains local, with its original colors and geometry. Source descriptions are embedded in corrected files.

## Color logo corrections on 2026-10-23

Monochrome fallbacks for Google Slides, Asana, Airtable, Dropbox, Google Analytics, Instagram, LinkedIn, Salesforce, Shopify, Supabase, WhatsApp and YouTube were replaced with original color SVG artwork from the Iconify SVG Logos collection. Google Slides and Instagram use Homarr Dashboard Icons, as Google Docs and Sheets already do. HubSpot retains the existing compact Simple Icons symbol in brand orange (#ff7a59). Exact collection identifiers and source are recorded in `SOURCES.json`. These assets are bundled locally and retain the source geometry and colors.
